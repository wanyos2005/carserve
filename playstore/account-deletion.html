DriveOn Account Deletion

Last Updated: October 26, 2025

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your DriveOn account and associated personal data as described below.

  1. Email us from your registered email address to privacy@driveon.com with the subject: “Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Include your full name and registered email in the body for verification.
  3. Optionally, mention any specific data (e.g., uploaded photos) you want removed sooner than standard retention timelines.

We’ll acknowledge your request within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days (or sooner, where feasible).

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile (email, name, phone, profile picture)
  • Vehicles & service history stored under your profile, subject to legal/contractual retention noted below
  • User-generated content (e.g., photos you uploaded)
  • Notifications tokens (FCM tokens) linked to your account

What May Be Retained (and Why)

  • Legal/Financial records related to completed services may be retained up to 7 years for compliance, auditing, and warranty purposes.
  • System logs and security records may be retained for a limited period to prevent fraud and ensure platform integrity.
  • Content that has been shared publicly or interacted with by others may remain in anonymized or aggregated form where complete removal would impact other users’ records.

Verification

To protect your privacy, we process deletion requests only from the email address associated with the account or after successful identity verification.

Partial Data Deletion

If you wish to remove specific data (for example, a particular uploaded photo), include details in your email and we will evaluate and process where feasible.
